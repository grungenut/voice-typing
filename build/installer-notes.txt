Voice Typing

Hold Right Alt, talk, let go: the words are typed where your cursor is.
Tap Right Ctrl to record a meeting; tap again to stop and get a transcript with speaker labels.

Everything runs on this PC. No account, no subscription, and no audio ever leaves the computer.

Good to know before you continue:

* The first start downloads the speech model (about 140 MB) once. A small pill near the
  bottom of the screen shows progress; a green "ready" pill means it is working.

* Right Alt and Right Ctrl are reserved for Voice Typing while it runs. Left Alt and
  Left Ctrl are unchanged.

* A microphone icon appears in the notification area (bottom right, near the clock).
  Click it for settings, your transcripts folder, the log, and Quit.

* Recording other people: many places require you to tell participants that a meeting is
  being recorded, and some require everyone's consent. Announce it at the start. You are
  responsible for following the law where you and the other participants are.

* Meeting recordings and transcripts are saved in Documents\Meeting Transcripts. They are
  ordinary files; delete them whenever you like.

* This program has no NVIDIA GPU support built in. On a PC with an NVIDIA card, the source
  version on GitHub runs a larger, faster model. For most people the difference is small.
